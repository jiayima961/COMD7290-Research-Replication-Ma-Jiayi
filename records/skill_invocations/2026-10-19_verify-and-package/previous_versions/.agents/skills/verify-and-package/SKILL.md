# verify-and-package

## Inputs

Original sources, approved specification, input versions, implementation code, actual outputs/logs, and the complete inventory. Inspect original evidence before relying on an implementation summary.

## Procedure

Check alignment from specification to code and outputs. Independently verify one important descriptive quantity and one central advanced-model quantity with a separately implemented calculation or method; state coverage. Demonstrate a harmless negative test on copied/synthetic inputs and recovery. Compare values, intervals, counts, variances, tests, diagnostics, and probabilities, not significance alone. Audit at least three consequential AI decisions with the student's acceptance/correction. Account for all inventory entries, a separate sensitivity analysis, follow-up design, and clean rerun. Prepare the APA 7 report, assessed tag/release checklist, and full commit only after the required research is actually complete.

## Outputs

outputs/comparison.csv, review_report.md, docs/limitations.md, records/decisions.md, checkpoints.md, and report/ materials, with evidence paths and commands. All project-authored narrative, templates, and filenames must be English.

## Validation

A second session rerunning the author script is not independent statistical verification. Empty folders and templates are not execution evidence. Verify permissions, coverage, the identical report, and the student's final acceptance. Record actual invocation, failures, and checks without prefilling success.

## Stopping rules

Do not declare completion or create a final assessed version without full actual results, independent checks, a clean rerun, and the student's final review. The current user instruction is to postpone the instructor invitation. Packaging must remain within the user's authorization at that stage.
