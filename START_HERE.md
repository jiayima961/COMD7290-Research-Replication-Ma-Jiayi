# Start here

This is the final repository reading route. Current documents come first.
Earlier versions and execution receipts remain in their dated record folders.

1. **Read the report and project overview.** Open the [final individual Word report](report/COMD7290%20Individual%20Report%20for%20Research%20Replication.docx), [README](README.md) and [final status](STATUS.md).
2. **Trace the theory to the models.** Read the [theory](docs/theory.md), [matrix guide](docs/hypothesis_matrix.md), [complete matrix](docs/hypothesis_matrix.csv), [dictionary](docs/variable_dictionary.md), [two model figures](docs/model_diagram.md) and [execution specification](docs/execution_specification.md).
3. **Inspect the results and comparison.** Read the [results explanation](docs/replication_results.md), [two technical Word reports](report/verification/README.md), [full ledger](outputs/verification/comparison_all.csv) and [hypothesis tests](outputs/reproduced/hypothesis_tests.csv).
4. **Inspect verification and research extensions.** Use the [test evidence](records/skill_tests/verify-and-package/TEST_REPORT.md), [independent checks](outputs/verification/independent_checks/checks.json), [country influence analysis](outputs/verification/sensitivity/README.md), [limitations](docs/limitations.md) and [follow-up design](docs/follow_up.md).
5. **Use the three skills.** Follow the [skill guide](docs/skills/README.md) from the repository root. The actual packages are under [.agents/skills](.agents/skills/).
6. **Review the student's contributions and decisions.** Follow [student v1](records/student_inputs/2026-10-18_initial_theory_explanation_v1.txt) → [feedback 1](docs/theory_feedback_v1.md) → [student v2](records/student_inputs/2026-10-18_revised_theory_explanation_v2.txt) → [feedback 2](docs/theory_feedback_v2.md), then inspect [decision reviews](records/skill3_decision_audit.md) and the [approved handoff](records/handoffs/2026-10-20/README.md).
7. **Rerun or continue from files.** Use the [teacher quickstart](docs/guides/teacher_quickstart.md), [source access](sources/README.md), [environment](environment.md), [analysis guide](analysis/README.md) and [receiving-session receipt](records/handoffs/2026-10-20/receiving_check.json).
8. **Check the work history.** Use the [records index](records/README.md), [finalization record](records/finalization/2026-10-20/README.md), [assignment audit](docs/assignment_audit.md) and [reorganization record](records/reorganization/2026-10-19/README.md). Historical receipts retain the state observed on their dates.

Preserve original inputs, prior student versions and completed execution evidence.
New research runs should use a separate dated record.
